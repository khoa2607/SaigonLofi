# Holdfast — PAST timeline: design decisions

Branch `design/holdfast-world`. Written before implementation; `STATUS.md` records what was actually built and measured.

The Past is the **same valley, the same map seed and the same community footprint** as the Current timeline: the river
meander, creek, wall arch, gate run, towers, house lots, civic sites, fields, docks, POIs and threat routes are generated by
the same code. Only what stands on that geography (art), who lives there (looks, tools) and what comes out of the forest
(threats) changes. It remains an observation-only living diorama.

## How it plugs in

- One flag, `HF.era` (`'current'` | `'past'`), chosen before the map is built (`?era=past`, or the PAST tab, which since milestone 8.1 crossfades to it in the page, no reload, and remembers it). Every era-specific branch checks it, so the Current timeline's code paths and random
  sequences are untouched.
- New files: `holdfast/hf-past-art.js` (prehistoric models, people, dinosaurs; DOM-free) and `holdfast/hf-past.js`
  (dinosaur behaviour, fire, rituals, torches, traps; DOM-free). Existing files get small hooks only.
- Reused unchanged: navigation grid, flow fields, task chains, schedules, stores, crops, hauling, cooking, hospital/school/
  farmyard/graveyard loops, expeditions, rescues, arrivals, river life, boats, weather, time of day, seasons, camera, shared UI
  and audio channels.

## What the major buildings become

| Current | Past |
|---|---|
| Suburban houses | Timber-and-wattle roundhouses / small longhouses with steep thatch, hide door flaps, smoke holes |
| Barracks + drill yard | Hunters' longhouse (antler crest), spear racks, a hide target range |
| Hospital + medical tent | Healer's lodge (herb bundles drying under the eaves, green-and-ochre painted hand sign), a healer's hide tent, fur pallets |
| Church (tallest building) | **Ancestor Hall**: a great timber hall with a very tall thatched roof and a carved **totem spire** with an antler crown — still the tallest structure; sacred fire bowl in front |
| Schoolhouse | **Story Lodge**: a round lodge with painted hide panels (hunting scenes, sun disc), a story circle of stumps, a children's spear-practice target |
| Workshop + forge | Craft yard: knapping hearth, flat working stones, hide-stretching frames, a travois instead of the wagon |
| Stable + horses | Beast pen: tamed small herbivore dinosaurs ("ridgebacks") instead of horses; the riders still scout |
| Kitchen + mess tent | Cookhouse with roasting spit and a big hide canopy over log tables |
| Market stalls | Barter mats under hide awnings |
| Graveyard | Burial ground of stone cairns and standing stones behind a wattle fence (the night spirits stay) |
| Water tower, turbines, greenhouses | Great signal beacon platform, totem poles, fish / herb drying racks |
| Street lamps, lanterns | Torches on poles (lit at dusk by a fire-keeper) |
| Cars, bus, wrecks, power poles, gas station, ruins | Boulders, fallen logs, a giant fossil skeleton, a rock shelter, standing stones, rocky outcrops |
| Sheet-metal wall, razor wire | **Palisade** of sharpened logs lashed with rope, stone footing, outward stakes |
| Watchtowers | Timber watch platforms with thatch roofs and **fire beacons** |
| Gate | Log gate under a lintel with antlers |
| Hedgehogs (kill zone) | Sharpened stake traps (they wound dinosaurs that blunder into them) |
| Sampans / medical boats | Dugout canoes and rafts: fish canoes, trade rafts, healer's canoes with herb baskets |

## Threats: dinosaurs instead of infected

Hostile species (each with colour morphs, so groups never look identical):

| Category | Species | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Small fast | **Compsognathus** ("compy") | Little packs that dart about the fields edge and harass livestock; weak |
| Pack hunters | **Velociraptor**, **Utahraptor** | Hunt in packs of 3-6, flank, very fast; the commonest attackers at the wall |
| Stalker | **Dilophosaurus** | Frilled; **circles and watches** a target before closing in |
| Chargers | **Pachycephalosaurus**, **Carnotaurus**, **Triceratops** | Sprint at the wall / gate and ram it (heavy damage on the first hit) |
| Wall breaker | **Ankylosaurus** | Slow, armoured, tail club smashes **weak sections** (the culvert, damaged logs) |
| Large predators | **Allosaurus**, **Spinosaurus** | Roam the band beyond the cleared ground, hunt alone, damage walls |
| Apex | **Tyrannosaurus rex** | Rare; roars (heard far, scatters herbivores), very hard to bring down, breaks the palisade |
| Flyer | **Pteranodon** | Flies over the wall: scouts, **swoops** on livestock and people in the fields, flees when hit |

Non-hostile herbivores (wildlife): **Parasaurolophus** herds (they take the deer's place: grazing, fleeing, hunted by
raptors and by hunting parties), lone **Stegosaurus** and the occasional giant **Brachiosaurus** far out in the wilderness.

## Humans

Tribal community: hides, furs and woven fibre in earthy colours, fur trims, bone and bead necklaces, headbands with feathers,
face paint for hunters, wraps on the feet, wild hair and braids. Hunters (the old guards) carry **spears and bows**; tower
watchers shoot **arrows** (fire arrows at night); expeditions become **hunting parties** that throw spears. No guns, no
modern gear, no red crosses, no printed signs.

## Fire

Fire is the Past's signature: torches on poles around the settlement (lit at dusk by a fire-keeper, out at dawn), house
campfires, a communal fire on the green, the sacred fire in front of the Ancestor Hall, the cookhouse spit, the knapping hearth,
**tower beacons** that blaze during alarms and at night, guards carrying torches on night patrols, fire arrows, and dinosaurs
that shy away from the flames (smaller species near a lit beacon at night may break off).

## Activity loops (reused systems, reinterpreted, plus new ones)

- Reused and re-skinned: planting / harvest of early grains and tubers, gathering into the store, cooking, fishing (bank and
  canoe), bathing / washing, hauling with a travois, palisade repairs, healer check-ups, story lodge lessons, ball games,
  pens (boars, fowl, goats), burial ground visits, barter market, beast tamers and riders, hunting parties (expeditions),
  rescues, newcomers.
- New: the **fire-keeper's torch round** at dusk, the **evening fire dance** at the Ancestor Hall, the sacred fire tended by
  elders, **beacons** lit in alarms, **stake traps**, flyers raiding the farmyard (and being chased off), herbivore herds.
